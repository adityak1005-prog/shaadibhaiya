How it works

Four steps, running continuously from the first vendor call to the last cab drop.

  1. 1

    Tell it the brief

    Date, city, guest count, budget and the kind of wedding you want. This becomes the shared state every agent works from.

  2. 2

    Agents build shortlists

    The recommendation agent and five department agents turn hundreds of vendor options into a handful worth your time.

  3. 3

    Shaadibhaiya runs the chat

    The WhatsApp agent handles vendor conversations, transcribes calls and voice notes, and confirms what was actually agreed.

  4. 4

    You stay the decision-maker

    The supervisor tracks budget and cross-department conflicts, and messages you the moment something needs your call — nothing books itself.

The one role that watches everything

The supervisor never makes a decision — it makes sure you can.

It sits above all eight agents, tracking the running budget and catching the moments one department's choice breaks another's — a decor plan that won't fit the venue's load-in time, a guest count the caterer wasn't told about. When something needs a human call, it sends you a supervisor alert on WhatsApp. It never books, cancels or chooses on your behalf.

Meet the squad

Eight specialists, one shared memory of your wedding.

Recommendation

Narrows every vendor category to a shortlist that actually fits your taste and budget — so you compare five options, not fifty.

Venue

Tracks availability, pricing and hold deadlines across every venue still in the running.

Catering

Manages tastings, menu revisions and dietary requirements as they change.

Decor

Keeps mood boards, quotes and install timelines lined up with the venue and the date.

Entertainment

Books and reconfirms your DJ, baraat band or performers as the schedule firms up.

Guests

Handles the guest list, RSVPs and seating logistics as numbers move around.

Scheduling

Builds the day-of timeline, and books the cabs for every pickup and drop around it.

WhatsApp

Reads vendor messages, voice notes and call recordings, and turns them into confirmed, written decisions.

Built for how Indian weddings actually run

Vendors go quiet — you don't have to chase

Every department keeps a backup vendor list on standby. If someone stops responding, a replacement is already waiting, not a fresh search.

It speaks fluent WhatsApp

Most vendor coordination in India happens as chat, voice notes and phone calls, not forms. Shaadibhaiya is built to work inside that, not around it.

It mediates, it doesn't decide

Every recommendation, booking and change comes back to you and your event planner for a yes. The system coordinates; you choose.
